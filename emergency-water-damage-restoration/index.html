When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Salt Lake City

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Walls on the lower floor are wet from top to bottom because of a leak overhead.
  • A water heater tank has failed and is emptying across the floor.
  • You came home to water and do not know how long it ran.
  • Water is still flowing and you cannot find or reach the shutoff valve.
  • Water is coming through a ceiling, light fixture or electrical outlet.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • Trapped water overhead has made part of the ceiling bulge or sag.

Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Pipes running through unheated areas need insulation to keep them from freezing and bursting.
  • Locate the main shutoff valve now, and show each person at home how to turn it off.
  • Run a check on the sump pump ahead of wet weather, and think about adding battery backup.
  • Know where your electrical panel is so power to wet rooms can be cut safely.
  • Keep the number of an emergency restoration company saved in your phone before you need it.
  • Swap worn hoses feeding the washer, sinks and toilets rather than waiting for a rupture.
